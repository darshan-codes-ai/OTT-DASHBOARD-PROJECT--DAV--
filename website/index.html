INTERPRETATION

Evidence-first insights

01

Catalog footprint

Prime Video carries the largest platform flag count in this dataset, with 4,113 records.

02

Release peak

The supplied data peaks at 2019 with 1,014 records in the year-count analysis.

03

Age mix

18+ is the largest non-missing age group at 2,276 records, while Age is missing in 4,177 rows.

04

High-rated representation

335 records meet the project threshold of RT ≥ 8.0/10. Raw platform counts are affected by catalog size and overlap.
