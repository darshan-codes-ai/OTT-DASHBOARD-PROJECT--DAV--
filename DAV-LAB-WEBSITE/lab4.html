Laboratories / Assignment 04

Interactive Data Visualization with Plotly

Explore Rotten Tomatoes ratings, release-year trends, age classifications, platform availability, and correlations through seven interactive visualizations.

Notebook: DAV_Lab_Assignment_4_Movie_Streaming_Plotly.ipynb
Primary Libraries: Plotly, Pandas, NumPy
Active Score Scale: Scale: 0–10 (Normalized)

Laboratory 4 transforms static analytical charts into fully interactive visual explorations. Using client-side Plotly.js rendered directly from the verified 9,515-title catalog, visitors can pan, zoom, inspect rich tooltips, filter age cohorts, and dynamically adjust range sliders.

Dataset & Transformation Protocol

All seven visualizations are dynamically generated from MoviesOnStreamingPlatforms.csv (9,515 deduplicated titles, 10 analytical columns). Rotten Tomatoes critic scores are normalized to a consistent 0–10 scale (e.g. 98/100 → 9.8), missing age certifications are labeled Missing/Unrated (4,177 titles), and platform co-licensing flags are calculated from the four provider columns (Netflix, Hulu, Prime Video, Disney+).
