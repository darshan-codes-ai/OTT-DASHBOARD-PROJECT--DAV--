Important Methodological Note: Platform Flags Are Non-Mutually Exclusive

In this dataset, platform presence is represented by binary indicators (1 or 0) across multiple columns. Because titles may be co-licensed and indexed simultaneously on more than one service, these counts represent catalog availability instances rather than partitioned unique movie titles.

Module 11

Key Analytical Takeaways (Viva Ready)

Core findings structured for viva examination, academic reporting, and presentation slides.

Executive Synthesis
01
Catalog Structure 97.34% Single-Platform

Near-Total Catalog Exclusivity

9,262 out of 9,515 titles (97.34%) are available on exactly one streaming service. Co-licensing across multiple platforms is exceptionally rare (only 253 multi-platform titles, and only 1 movie present on all 4). This confirms that movie streaming availability in this catalog is heavily partitioned by provider.

02
Quality vs Volume Raw Count vs Proportion

Catalog Volume vs. High-Rated Concentration

While Prime Video possesses the largest overall catalog (4,113 movies), Netflix holds the highest raw volume of high-rated titles (168 titles rated ≥ 8.0/10). However, Disney+ exhibits the highest concentration of high-rated titles relative to its catalog size (75 / 922 = 8.13%), compared to Netflix (4.55%), Hulu (4.68%), and Prime Video (1.29%).

03
Data Hygiene 43.9% Missing Values

Age Classification Data Sparsity

4,177 out of 9,515 movies (43.90%) lack an age classification certificate in the master dataset. Among rated titles, the 18+ tier is the largest (2,276 titles), followed by 7+ (1,090 titles). This high proportion of unrated titles highlights the importance of accounting for missing data when analyzing content ratings.

04
Temporal Distribution Peak Year: 2019

Recent Release Clustering

The catalog spans 107 years (1914–2021) with an average release year of 2007.42 and median of 2014. Content volume escalates sharply after 2010, reaching an all-time peak in 2019 with 1,014 titles before dropping slightly in 2020 (862) and 2021 (327).

05
Critic Distribution Mean 5.35 / Median 5.20

Central Tendency in Rotten Tomatoes Ratings

Critic scores conform closely to a unimodal bell-shaped distribution centered at a mean of 5.35/10 and a median of 5.20/10. 57.1% of all movies (5,429 titles) cluster within the mid-range 4.0–6.0 rating bin, with only 3.52% achieving scores ≥ 8.0/10.
