Important Score Scale Specification Note

In academic evaluations, score scale consistency is critical. Notice that Lab 1 and Lab 2 normalize the Rotten Tomatoes score to a 0–10 scale (e.g. 9.8 / 10, mean: 5.35), whereas the original Lab 3 Jupyter notebook maintains the un-normalized 0–100 scale (e.g. 98 / 100, mean: 53.55). To preserve authentic laboratory provenance, each laboratory article on this website faithfully respects its notebook's original scale, prominently highlighted with visual badges.
