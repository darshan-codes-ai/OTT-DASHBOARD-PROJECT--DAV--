Academic Methodology Note: Availability Flags vs. Unique Movies

Summing the platform totals yields 9,777 availability instances (Prime Video: 4,113 + Netflix: 3,695 + Hulu: 1,047 + Disney+: 922). However, because licensing agreements can overlap across services, the actual dataset contains 9,515 unique movie titles. Specifically, 245 titles are simultaneously hosted on 2 services, 7 titles on 3 services, and 1 title (Zapped, 2014) is available across all 4 services.
